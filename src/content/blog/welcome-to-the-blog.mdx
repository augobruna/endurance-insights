For two seasons we've poured everything into the podcast — long conversations
with coaches, athletes, scientists, and everyday humans chasing extraordinary
things. The audio will always be the main thing we make.

But there's a lot that doesn't fit into an episode: training notes, race
reports, the article-length versions of conversations, references and reading
lists. That's what this blog is for.

## What you'll find here

- **Episode companions** — written deep-dives that go with podcast interviews
- **Race reports** — what actually happened, what we learned, what we'd do differently
- **Training notes** — the practical stuff we use with our own athletes
- **Reading lists** — the books, papers, and people shaping how we think

## What's next

New posts will land here first, then get cross-posted to
[Substack](https://justbrunathings.substack.com/) for email subscribers. If you
want them in your inbox, subscribe there. If you want them as they go live,
bookmark this page.

Thanks for being here.

— Bruna
